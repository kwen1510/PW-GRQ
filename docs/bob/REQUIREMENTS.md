# pw-grq requirements

Status: **owner-directed prototype scope recorded; institutional approval remains external**

The product supports allowlisted RI teachers conducting PW group interviews. The accepted prototype behavior is local-first capture and retention: recordings, transcripts, sessions and analyses remain within the verified teacher's browser profile, can be replayed/exported/deleted there, and are not synchronised to a central session repository. OpenAI receives only the content necessary for teacher-requested transcription or analysis. Shared prompt configuration, quotas and transient idempotency metadata may remain server-side; they are not teacher session records.

Required analysis behavior: readable question-level headings, a clean iPad-responsive interface, evidence-grounded strengths and gaps, explicit suggested content/research areas, student-specific next steps, evidence limitations, local history retrieval and an individual report download.

Rejected for this release: central storage or cross-device sharing of teacher sessions, transcripts, audio or analyses. A future central repository requires a new explicit owner decision and participant/institutional authorization.
